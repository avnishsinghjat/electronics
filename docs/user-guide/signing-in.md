# Signing in and your account

## First sign-in

Your administrator gives you a username and a **temporary password**. After you sign in
with it, FabTrack asks you to choose your own password before anything else.

Password rules: at least 10 characters, a mix of letters with digits or symbols, and it
must not contain your username.

## Staying signed in

You stay signed in on that browser for up to 7 days of inactivity. Reloading the page does
not sign you out. Use **Sign out** in the account menu (top right) on shared PCs and
tablets.

## Locked out?

After 5 wrong passwords in a row the account locks for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask an
administrator to reset your password (that also unlocks it).

## Changing your password

Account menu → **Change password**. Your other devices are signed out.

## What you can see

Your **roles** decide which sections appear in the sidebar and which buttons you see. If
your account is restricted to certain divisions or projects, other projects simply do not
appear. Ask an administrator if you need more access.
